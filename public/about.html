THEWHY Consulting is a boutique management consulting firm headquartered in Yaba, Lagos. The firm was established by seasoned chartered accountants and turnaround practitioners with a clear mission: to resolve high-stakes commercial dilemmas that standard business advisories fail to cure.

We do not believe in the recycling of textbook templates. In the volatile Nigerian business climate, survival demands acute versatility, forensic financial insight, and operational pragmatism. Whether navigating bank receiverships, restructuring balance sheets, or mentoring next-generation directors, our focus remains squarely on bottom-line outcomes.

Through our direct operational affiliation with Wale Kehinde & Co. (Chartered Accountants), our clients enjoy seamless access to statutory audit verification, legal tax representation before FIRS and LIRS, and accredited training credentials recognized across Nigeria.

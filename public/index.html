Our Story

Practical Solutions to Real Scenarios.

Headquartered in Yaba, Lagos, THEWHY Consulting was established to provide practical, lasting business remedies where standard approaches fail. We do not recycle generic slide decks or one-size-fits-all strategies. Our belief is grounded in operational adaptability, standing beside enterprise leaders to navigate insolvency, tax disputes, and structural growth.

As an affiliate of Wale Kehinde & Co. (Chartered Accountants), our advisory is backed by decades of accredited accounting stewardship, statutory auditing authority, and formal representation before the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) and State Internal Revenue Services.

Mr. Kehinde Adewale, FCA, ACIT, MBA Managing Consultant • Wale Kehinde & Co. Practice Partner

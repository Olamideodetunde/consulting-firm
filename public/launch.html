Structured Roadmap

6-Week Executive Curriculum

Week 01

Financial Diagnostics & Cloud Treasury Architecture

Stress-testing balance sheets, identifying hidden cash flow drains, algorithmic bank charge audits, and establishing cloud accounting standards.

Week 02

Tax Optimization & Statutory Approvals (TCC / PENCOM)

Navigating CITA, VAT, and withholding taxes, legal tax planning incentives, and procuring PENCOM, ITF, and NSITF procurement certificates.

Week 03

Corporate Finance & Sourcing Affordable Capital

Preparing bankable project reports, debt turnaround frameworks, evaluating buy-or-lease decisions, and attracting private equity.

Week 04

AI Integration & Operational Automation

Deploying generative AI and workflow automations to streamline customer acquisition, financial reporting, and compliance monitoring.

Week 05

Corporate Governance & Founder Succession

Building independent advisory boards, maintaining statutory registers, drafting executive compensation plans, and creating continuity charters.

Week 06

Scale-Up Strategy, Mergers & Capstone Presentation

Joint ventures, M&A readiness, executing the final strategic turnaround plan, and certification award ceremony.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this programme tailored for?

Business owners, CEOs, Managing Directors, and Finance Controllers of established SMEs operating with annual turnover from ₦25M to ₦2B+ who want to scale into enduring institutions.

Is the training certified by statutory bodies?

Yes. All training is fully certified by the Centre for Management Development (CMD), qualifying participants for mandatory professional development credits and procurement bids.

What is the format and duration?

The programme runs for 6 weeks in a flexible hybrid format: weekend executive workshops at our Lagos training facility and weekly virtual interactive strategy masterclasses.

How do I register or sponsor team members?

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

THEWHY Consulting is committed to maintaining the highest professional standards of confidentiality, data integrity, and compliance under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and global best practices.

1. Introduction & Commitment

THEWHY Consulting ("we", "our", or "us"), an affiliate of Wale Kehinde & Co. (Chartered Accountants), respects the privacy of our clients, website visitors, and business partners. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard corporate and personal information obtained through our website (why.ng), advisory consultations, and executive development programmes.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver high-quality management consulting, accounting, tax, and corporate finance services:

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full name, executive title, business email, telephone/WhatsApp number, and corporate entity name.
  • Business Diagnostic Data: Nature of enterprise, turnover bracket, statutory status, and specific consulting advisory requirements submitted via our enquiry or programme forms.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and anonymous interaction analytics used to enhance website responsiveness and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate professional purposes:

  • Responding to executive inquiries, diagnostic requests, and consultation bookings.
  • Evaluating applications for THEWHY SME Executive Strategic Bootcamp (CMD-Certified).
  • Preparing engagement proposals, non-disclosure agreements, and regulatory advisory memorandums.
  • Complying with statutory reporting requirements under Nigerian tax law and professional chartered accounting guidelines.

4. Confidentiality & Data Security

As professional consultants and chartered accountants, client confidentiality is central to our fiduciary duty. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. All financial statements, tax records, and internal disclosures provided during engagements are protected by strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) and stored in encrypted, access-controlled repositories.

5. Your Data Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request rectification of inaccurate records, or request erasure of your data where statutory retention obligations do not apply.

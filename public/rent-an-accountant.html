Our Deliverables

Bookkeeping & Reconciliations

Day-to-day recording of sales, purchases and expenses, with monthly bank and ledger reconciliations.

Management Accounts

Monthly balance sheets, cash flow reports and monthly Profit or Loss statements you can use to make decisions.

Statutory Compliance

Real-time remittances of PAYE, WHT, VAT, pension and other statutory deductions.

NRS & LIRS Tax Filings

Company income tax, VAT, WHT and personal income tax returns, plus support whenever a tax authority raises a query.

Year-End & CAC Annual Returns

Year-end schedules for your auditors, and filing your CAC annual returns so your company stays in good standing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the accountant work on-site?

We offer flexible working arrangements to suit your business. Your accountant can work remotely, on-site at your office, or through a hybrid arrangement, depending on your needs.

Who reviews the accountant’s work?

Our senior accounting team reviews accounts and tax filings for accuracy and compliance.

Can you help clear a backlog of unprocessed records?

Yes. We can update your records before transitioning to ongoing accounting support.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on your business needs and agreed scope. Once the engagement letter is signed, we provide a clear, fixed fee with no hidden charges.

Why Rent an Accountant

The Finance Team You Need, Without the Overhead.

Most growing businesses need reliable accounting long before they can justify a full finance department. We place a qualified accountant with your business, on-site or remotely, and our senior consultants review the work, so your records are accurate, your filings are on time and you always know where you stand.

Lower cost than recruiting and managing a full-time hire

What's Covered

What Your Accountant Handles

Bookkeeping & Reconciliations

Day-to-day recording of sales, purchases and expenses, with monthly bank and ledger reconciliations.

Management Accounts

Monthly profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow reports you can use to make decisions.

Payroll, PAYE & Pensions

Accurate monthly payroll, PAYE deductions and timely pension and statutory remittances.

VAT & WHT Returns

Computing, filing and remitting VAT and withholding tax on time, with clean supporting schedules.

NRS & LIRS Tax Filings

Company and personal income tax returns, plus support whenever a tax authority raises a query.

Year-End & CAC Annual Returns

Year-end schedules for your auditors, and filing your CAC annual returns so your company stays in good standing.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the accountant work on-site?

Either. Your accountant can work from your office on agreed days, remotely on your cloud accounting system, or a mix of both.

Who checks the accountant's work?

Every engagement is overseen by our senior consultants, who review the monthly accounts and filings before they reach you.

Can you start with a backlog of unprocessed records?

Yes. Many clients start with a project to bring their books up to date, then move to a monthly part-time or dedicated arrangement.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on your transaction volume and the scope of work. After the free consultation we send a fixed monthly quote, with no surprises.

What You'll Learn

Practical Answers to the Questions Business Leaders Ask Us Most

Tax Compliance Under the NRS

What the move from FIRS to the Nigeria Revenue Service means for your business, how to stay compliant and how to respond to assessments.

CAC Registration & Compliance

Choosing the right structure, registering correctly, and keeping annual returns and statutory filings up to date with the CAC.

Funding & Bankable Structure

How lenders and investors assess a business, and how to prepare records and reports that make your company fundable.

Modern Bookkeeping & Automation

Moving from spreadsheets to cloud accounting, and using simple automation to cut routine finance and reporting work.

People, Payroll & Statutory Clearance

Sound HR policies, payroll and PAYE, and meeting PENCOM, ITF and NSITF obligations so you qualify for contracts and tenders.

Peer Network & Open Q&A

Time with fellow business owners and THEWHY's senior consultants to put your own questions on the table.
